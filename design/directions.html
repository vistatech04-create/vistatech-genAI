Same colours. Same words. Three different feelings.

Every route below uses the colours straight out of the logo, and the exact same sentences, so the only thing you are judging is the look. All three are light-led. Each one uses dark in exactly one place, and I have marked where.

Route A

The notebook

Everything sits on paper. Sections are separated by lines, not boxes. Gold behaves like a highlighter pen and orange like a pen underline. There are no dark blocks anywhere — dark is only ever the text. Calm, honest, a little bit academic.
