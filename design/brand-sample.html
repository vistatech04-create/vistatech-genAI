The system behind those three sections

Every decision, in one place

Colour — pulled from the arrow

Ink#280820Arrow body. All text and borders.
Plum#452038Cards on dark bands.
Paper#F7F2EAThe page ground.
Sand#EDE4D6The alternate band.
Flame#F85830Arrow tips. Buttons only.
Gold#D8B820The peak. Highlights, never a button.
Green#1E7A4FMoney saved. Nothing else.
The logo has three warm colours, but a page can only have one action colour. Flame is it — if it's flame, it's clickable. Gold marks and highlights, never clicks. That way a person's eye always knows where to go next.

Buttons

Buttons are 54px tall and full width on a phone, so a thumb can't miss them. Flame with dark text, not white text — white on orange is hard to read in sunlight, and a lot of your students will open this outdoors on a phone.

The shadow is a solid offset block, not a blur — it's one square sitting behind another, exactly how the logo is built. It also renders sharp on cheap Android screens, where soft shadows turn into grey mud. Corners: 20px on cards, 999px on buttons.
